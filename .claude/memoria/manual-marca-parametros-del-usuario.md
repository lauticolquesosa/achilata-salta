The Achilata brand manual is in `02_Marca/Manual de marca/Achilata Manual de Marca.ai`. It has 33 A4 landscape artboards, and the user's 4 original guideline artboards are at the end on a locked layer. There is a PDF and one PNG per page in `Exportes/`. It was built on 2026-11-18 with ExtendScript through COM from PowerShell (`New-Object -ComObject Illustrator.Application`, `DoJavaScript`).

The user's parameters must not change:
- Logo: `achilata.` with drips.
- Colors: #E7364A, #FFF3E2, #F3A3B5, #FFFFFF, #3A1320, #000000.
- Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold for titles, Regular for text, Ink Free only for details.
- The two drip patterns, used only on special occasions.
- The drip backgrounds.

**Why:** The user designs the brand themselves. My job is to complete it and polish it to a professional standard, using the brandbooks in their agency as reference (`Documents/Mi Agencia - Identidad de Marca/biblioteca/brandbooks`, mainly La Vaca).

**How to apply:** Every post, story or reel follows the manual and `04_Contenido/reglas_de_contenido.md`. Related: [[proyecto-achilata-salta]], [[reglas-contenido-achilata]]
