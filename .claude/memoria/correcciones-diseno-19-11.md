On 2026-11-19 the user disliked the first P01 and H01 ("no me parecen muy lindas") and set these rules for good. They override the brand manual:

- **Bordó #3A1320 only for tiny details,** never as a background or a block. Backgrounds are Achilata red #E7364A with white text. Crema is an occasional background.
- **Titles get more weight.** Add a stroke in the same color to ExtraBold titles so they look wider, at 2 to 4 % of the font size.
- **Carousels carry photos on almost every slide,** with few flat color slides, and the whole carousel shares one look.
- **Never use the reduced `a.` logo.** Always the full logo without the tagline.
- **Photo editing is the core of every piece,** at Rocambolesc level, and the copy matters just as much.
- **Research and analysis come first,** in depth, before redesigning.

**Why:** The user wants pieces that are much more aesthetic, almost artistic, and done like a senior graphic designer.

**How to apply:** Apply to every piece, together with [[reglas-contenido-achilata]] and [[flujo-piezas-a-pedido]].
